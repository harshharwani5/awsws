Key Milestones

A Decade of Driving Change

Education

Padhega Bachpan Badhega Bharat

Distributed 5,000+ school kits and established 10 evening learning centers for tribal children who previously lacked access to basic schooling.

Outcome: 40% reduction in local primary school dropout rates.
Community Healthcare

Rural Health Camps: Doctor Consultations at Doorsteps

Bringing experienced volunteer doctors, blood pressure screenings, eye tests, and free medicines to remote colonies where clinics are miles away.

Outcome: Over 120 camps completed with 15,000+ free medical tests conducted.
