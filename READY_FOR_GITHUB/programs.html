The 10 Flagship Program Domains of AESWS

Action-driven social welfare projects spanning education, women's empowerment, healthcare camps, organic biotechnology, tribal heritage preservation, and micro-entrepreneurship across Madhya Pradesh.

Domain 1: Education
Free Children's Libraries & Bridge Schools

1. Padhega Bachpan, Badhega Bharat

Operating free children's study libraries, providing non-formal remedial schooling, school kits, uniforms, and digital tablets to prevent dropouts in underserved slum clusters.

  • Free textbooks, stationery kits & daily mid-day meals
  • 5,400+ children supported in foundational numeracy & literacy
5,400+ EnrolledTarget: 10,000
Sponsor a Child's Education
Domain 2: Girl Child
Self-Defense & Higher Studies

2. Betiyon Ki Udaan

Specialized mentorship for girl children from marginalized rural families providing scholarships, career counseling, menstrual hygiene workshops, and martial arts self-defense training.

  • Self-defense & cyber safety workshops for adolescent girls
  • Merit scholarship support for higher secondary retention
2,200+ Girls MentoredTarget: 5,000
Support a Girl Child
Domain 3: Women SHGs
NMDFC Sanctioned Unit (₹8.00 Lakhs)

3. Shashakt Matrashakti & Swachh Saheli

Women empowerment through Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and the NMDFC (Ministry of Minority Affairs) sanctioned "Swachh Saheli – Sanitary Pad Manufacturing Unit" in Vidisha providing sustainable incomes.

  • Sanitary pad production & distribution cooperative
  • Micro-credit linkage for rural women-led microenterprises
3,500+ SHG WomenTarget: 5,000
Sponsor a Mother's Kit
Domain 4: Health
Vridh Swasthya Shivir & Malnutrition Sattu

4. Swasthya Disha

Free primary health screening camps, specialized senior citizen health camps (Vridh Swasthya Shivir), eye checkups, cataract diagnosis, and high-protein roasted gram sattu distribution against malnutrition.

  • 120+ diagnostic health & blood donation camps completed
  • Free doctor consultations, blood pressure & diabetes screenings
120+ Camps HeldGoal: 200
Fund a Medical Camp
Domain 5: Relief
Winter Relief & Ration Distribution

5. Seva Sankalp

Emergency food ration distribution, clean drinking water kiosks in summers, and large-scale winter warmth drives distributing heavy blankets to pavement dwellers and daily-wage laborers.

  • 25,000+ heavy winter blankets distributed across MP
  • Emergency grocery kits for destitute families
25,000+ BlanketsGoal: 35,000
Donate Family Rations
Domain 6: Ecology
MPCST & Watershed Restoration

6. Green Kal & Millet Bio-Tech

Environmental conservation through massive plantation drives, watershed management training, and organic millet biotechnology cultivation workshops (in partnership with MP Council of Science & Technology).

  • 50,000+ saplings planted across barren district zones
  • Bio-fertilizer & organic farming training for rural farmers
50,000+ TreesTarget: 75,000
Sponsor a Tree
Domain 7: Youth
Rural Tech Empowerment

7. Digital Disha

Bridging the digital divide by establishing basic computer literacy centers in deep rural pockets. Training youth in basic IT, typing, government portal usage, and online banking safety.

  • Cyber hygiene and cashless transaction workshops
  • Free basic computer courses for marginalized youth
3,000+ Youth TrainedGoal: 5,000
Fund a Digital Lab
Domain 8: Arts
CFPG Scheme (Ministry of Culture)

8. Sanskriti Jyoti

Preserving vanishing Indian folk arts and tribal heritage. Supported by the Ministry of Culture (Govt. of India) under the CFPG scheme, empowering local artisans through exhibitions and grants.

  • Tribal craft and folk-dance preservation workshops
  • Livelihood support for traditional artisan families
25+ Artisan ClustersGoal: 30
Support Local Artisans
Domain 9: Livelihood
MPCON Skill Incubation (Govt. of India)

9. Udyam Udaan Incubation

Advanced livelihood and entrepreneurship hubs established in partnership with MPCON Limited. Training marginalized individuals in market-ready trades such as advanced garment manufacturing and retail.

  • State-of-the-art tailoring and apparel training labs
  • Direct market linkages and job placement assistance
1,500+ EmployedGoal: 2,000
Sponsor a Trainee
Domain 10: Social
Traffic Police Nukkad Natak & Nasha Mukti

10. Social Impact & Awareness

Active community interventions including Road Safety Nukkad Natak with Bhopal Traffic Police at 10 No. Market, Gram Chaupal dispute resolution, and Nasha Mukti (anti-addiction) drives in Renipaani & Chasharia.

  • Nasha Mukti counseling & youth anti-tobacco pledges
  • Work orders from Nagar Parishad Shahganj, Sanchi & Budhni
