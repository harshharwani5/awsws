About Alveera Education & Social Welfare Society

Registered on 01-07-2011 under MP Society Registration Act 1973 (Reg. No. 01/07/04/23424/11), dedicated to sustainable livelihood incubation, child education, women SHGs, environmental conservation, and social welfare across Madhya Pradesh.

Our Journey Since 2011

15+ Years of Dedicated Grassroots Service

Alveera Education & Social Welfare Society (अलवीरा एजुकेशन एण्ड सोशल वेलफेयर सोसाइटी) was founded in 2011 by visionary social leaders led by Shahana Parveen Khan with a mission to bring institutional governance and authentic grassroots execution to community welfare.

Over the last 15 years, AESWS has executed major government-sanctioned programs with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), NMDFC (Ministry of Minority Affairs), MPCST (MP Council of Science & Technology), Ministry of Culture, MPCON Limited, and MP Jan Abhiyan Parishad as a designated Navankur Sanstha.

Our Vision

An inclusive, empowered society where every underprivileged child, rural woman, and youth achieves self-reliance with dignity.

Our Mission

Execute market-aligned vocational training, free child libraries, primary healthcare vans, and green restoration.
